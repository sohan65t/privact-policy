Privacy Policy

Spend Vix Expense Tracker · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Summary

Your data stays on your device

Spend Vix Expense Tracker (“Spend Vix”, “the app”, “we”) is an offline expense tracker. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

1. Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive any information from the app. We have no servers that store your data and no way to see what you enter.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only in local storage on your phone:

  • Expenses you add (amount, category, payment method, note, date and time)
  • Monthly and category budgets
  • Custom categories
  • Preferences such as currency, dark/light theme and whether the Quick Expense notification is on

This data is kept in the app’s private storage and is not accessible to the developer or to other apps.

3. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy it is needed
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)Shows the “Quick Expense” shortcut notification in your notification shade and lock screen.
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, special use)Keeps the Quick Expense shortcut notification available. It does no background data collection, location, media or syncing.
Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)Restores the shortcut notification after your device restarts or the app is updated.

The app does not request access to your contacts, SMS, location, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it does not request internet access.

4. Shared payment messages

If you use Android’s Share option to send a payment confirmation message from another app (for example a UPI or wallet app) to Spend Vix, the text is analysed only on your device to detect the amount, date and merchant. It is not uploaded, stored on any server, or shared. The app only sees the text you choose to share; it does not read your messages in the background.

5. Export and backup

  • Export CSV: the file is written to a location you choose using Android’s file picker. Once exported, you control that file and anyone you share it with.
  • Android Auto Backup: depending on your device settings, Android may back up the app’s data to your own Google account and restore it to a new device. This is managed by Google and your device, under Google’s privacy policy, not by the developer. You can turn it off in your Android settings.

6. Third parties

Spend Vix does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party services that collect data, and it does not share information with any third party.

7. Data security

Because your data never leaves your device, its security depends on your device’s protection. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device up to date.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete all expenses from within the app: Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Remove everything, including budgets and settings, by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.
  • Turn off the shortcut notification in Settings → Quick Expense Notification, or in Android’s notification settings.

9. Children’s privacy

Spend Vix is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address and in the app (Settings → Privacy Policy).
